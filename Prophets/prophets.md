# The Prophets: From Adam (AS) to Muhammad (SAW)

A chronological account of the Prophets and Messengers mentioned in the Quran and authentic Hadith, with source references.

> **PBUH** = Peace Be Upon Him (Arabic: عليه السلام / AS)  
> **SAW** = Sallallahu Alayhi Wa Sallam (May Allah's peace and blessings be upon him)  
> Sources are strictly from the **Quran** and **authentic (Sahih) Hadith** collections.

---

## Preamble

Allah states in the Quran:

> *"Indeed, We have revealed to you as We revealed to Nuh and the prophets after him. And We revealed to Ibrahim, Ismail, Ishaq, Yaqub, the descendants, Isa, Ayyub, Yunus, Harun, and Sulayman. And to Dawud We gave the Zabur. And [We sent] messengers about whom We have related [their stories] to you before and messengers about whom We have not related to you. And Allah spoke to Musa with [direct] speech."*  
> — **Quran 4:163-164**

The Prophet Muhammad (SAW) said:

> *"The prophets are like brothers from one father, their mothers are different, but their religion is one."*  
> — **Sahih al-Bukhari 3443, Sahih Muslim 2365**

It is also narrated:

> *"Allah sent one hundred and twenty-four thousand prophets, among whom three hundred and fifteen were messengers (rasul)."*  
> — **Musnad Ahmad, classified as Sahih by Al-Albani**

The Quran names **25 prophets** explicitly. This document covers each of them in chronological order.

---

## 1. Adam (عليه السلام) — Adam

**The First Human and First Prophet**

Allah created Adam from clay and bestowed upon him knowledge, making him the first human and the first prophet.

- **Creation:** Allah created Adam from sounding clay like pottery. *Quran 55:14*
- **Knowledge:** Allah taught Adam the names of all things, and when the angels were unable to do so, Adam demonstrated his knowledge. *Quran 2:31-33*
- **The Prostration:** Allah commanded the angels to prostrate to Adam. All obeyed except Iblis (Satan), who refused out of arrogance. *Quran 2:34, 7:11-12, 15:28-31, 17:61, 18:50, 20:116*
- **The Fall:** Adam and Hawwa (Eve) were placed in Jannah and permitted to eat from all trees except one. Iblis deceived them, and they ate from the forbidden tree. *Quran 2:35-36, 7:19-22, 20:120-121*
- **Repentance:** Adam received words from his Lord and repented. Allah accepted his repentance. *Quran 2:37*
- **Descent to Earth:** Adam and Hawwa were sent down to Earth as a test. *Quran 2:38, 7:24, 20:123*
- **Status:** Adam is described as one chosen by Allah. *Quran 3:33*

**Sources:** Quran 2:30-39, 7:11-27, 15:26-44, 17:61-65, 18:50, 20:115-124, 38:71-85; Sahih al-Bukhari 3326 (creation from clay), 3331 (height of 60 cubits)

---

## 2. Idris (عليه السلام) — Enoch

**The First to Write with a Pen**

Idris is the second prophet mentioned in the Quran. He is identified by most scholars with the Biblical Enoch.

- **Elevated Status:** Allah mentions Idris as a truthful prophet and says He raised him to a high station. *Quran 19:56-57*
- **First to Write:** The Prophet (SAW) said: *"The first to write with a pen was Idris."* — **Musnad Ahmad, graded Sahih by Al-Albani**
- **Patience and Righteousness:** He is described among the patient and the righteous. *Quran 21:85, 38:2*
- **The Ascent:** Allah says *"We raised him to a high station"* — *Quran 19:57*. In a hadith, the Prophet (SAW) passed by Idris during the Night Journey (Isra and Mi'raj), and Idris greeted him. — **Sahih al-Bukhari 3207**

**Sources:** Quran 19:56-57, 21:85, 38.2; Sahih al-Bukhari 3207; Musnad Ahmad

---

## 3. Nuh (عليه السلام) — Noah

**The First Great Messenger**

Nuh is considered the first messenger sent after Adam. He preached for an extraordinarily long time — 950 years — with only a small number of followers.

- **The Mission:** Nuh was sent to his people to call them to worship Allah alone. *Quran 7:59, 11:25*
- **Duration:** He called his people for 950 years. *Quran 29:14*
- **Rejection:** His people rejected him, mocked him, and threatened to stone him. *Quran 7:60, 11:27, 26:116, 71:5-7*
- **The Ark:** Allah commanded Nuh to build the Ark. *Quran 11:37, 23:27, 54:13-14*
- **The Flood:** Allah sent the great flood, drowning the disbelievers. *Quran 7:64, 11:40-43, 23:27, 26:119-120, 29:14, 54:11-15, 69:10-12*
- **The Survivors:** Only Nuh and his family (except his son and wife who disbelieved) and the believers were saved. *Quran 11:40, 66:10, 71:26-27*
- **Status:** Nuh is among the five greatest prophets (Ulul 'Azm — those of determination). *Quran 42:13, 46:35*
- **Greeting from Muhammad (SAW):** During the Night Journey, Nuh greeted the Prophet (SAW) saying: *"Welcome to the righteous brother and righteous prophet."* — **Sahih al-Bukhari 3207**

**Sources:** Quran 7:59-64, 10:71-73, 11:25-49, 21:76-77, 23:23-30, 26:105-122, 29:14-15, 37:75-82, 54:9-16, 57:26, 66:10, 71:1-28; Sahih al-Bukhari 3207, 3340, 4400

---

## 4. Hud (عليه السلام) — Hud

**Prophet to the People of 'Ad**

Hud was sent to the ancient people of 'Ad, a powerful and tall nation in southern Arabia (likely modern-day Yemen/Oman).

- **The People of 'Ad:** A mighty, wealthy nation who built lofty buildings and were physically powerful. *Quran 7:69, 26:128-129, 41:15, 89:6-8*
- **The Message:** Hud called them to worship Allah alone and warned against arrogance. *Quran 7:65, 11:50, 26:124*
- **Rejection:** They called him a liar, a fool, and accused him of madness. *Quran 7:66, 11:53, 26:136, 46:22*
- **The Punishment:** Allah sent a devastating wind that destroyed them. *Quran 41:16, 46:24-25, 51:41, 54:19, 69:6-7*
- **Survival:** Hud and the believers were saved by Allah's mercy. *Quran 7:72, 11:58*

**Sources:** Quran 7:65-72, 11:50-60, 26:123-140, 41:13-16, 46:21-26, 51:41-42, 53:50, 54:18-22, 69:4-8, 89:6-8

---

## 5. Salih (عليه السلام) — Salih

**Prophet to the People of Thamud**

Salih was sent to Thamud, the successors of 'Ad, who carved homes in mountains and were skilled builders.

- **The People of Thamud:** A prosperous nation after 'Ad, known for carving dwellings in rock. *Quran 7:74, 15:82, 26:149*
- **The She-Camel:** Salih's people demanded a miracle. Allah sent a she-camel as a sign, and they were told to let her drink from their water on alternate days. *Quran 7:73, 11:64, 26:155, 54:27*
- **The Killing of the Camel:** The most wicked among them slaughtered the she-camel despite the warning. *Quran 7:77, 11:65, 26:157, 54:29*
- **The Punishment:** A terrible blast (sayhah) and earthquake destroyed them. *Quran 7:78, 11:67, 15:83, 27:51, 41:13-14, 51:44, 54:31, 69:5*
- **The Nine Wicked Men:** Nine men in the city plotted to kill Salih and the camel. *Quran 27:48*

**Sources:** Quran 7:73-79, 11:61-68, 15:80-84, 26:141-159, 27:45-53, 41:13-18, 51:43-45, 54:23-31, 69:4-5, 91:11-15

---

## 6. Ibrahim (عليه السلام) — Abraham

**Khalilullah — The Close Friend of Allah**

Ibrahim is one of the greatest prophets and the patriarch of monotheism. He is the forefather of many prophets through his sons Ismail and Ishaq.

- **Search for Truth:** As a young man, Ibrahim reasoned his way to monotheism by observing the stars, moon, and sun, realizing none could be God. *Quran 6:74-79*
- **Breaking the Idols:** Ibrahim destroyed the idols of his people except the largest, to demonstrate their powerlessness. *Quran 21:57-58, 37:91-95*
- **The Fire:** His people threw him into a fire, but Allah commanded it to be cool and safe for him. *Quran 21:68-69, 29:24, 37:97-98*
- **Debate with Nimrod:** Ibrahim debated the tyrant king who claimed divinity. *Quran 2:258*
- **Migration:** Ibrahim left his people and migrated to the blessed land. *Quran 19:48-49, 37:99, 29:26*
- **The Sacrifice:** Ibrahim saw in a dream that he was sacrificing his son (identified in Islamic tradition as Ismail). He and his son both submitted to Allah's command. Allah ransomed the son with a great sacrifice. *Quran 37:102-107*
- **The Building of the Ka'bah:** Ibrahim and Ismail built the Ka'bah in Makkah and prayed for it to be a place of worship. *Quran 2:125-127, 22:26*
- **The Prayers:** Ibrahim made numerous supplications recorded in the Quran — for Makkah, his descendants, and the sending of a messenger from among them. *Quran 2:126-129, 14:35-41, 26:83-89*
- **Status:** Ibrahim is called *Khalilullah* (the Close Friend of Allah). *Quran 4:125*. He is among the Ulul 'Azm. He is described as an *ummah* (a nation) unto himself. *Quran 16:120*

**Sources:** Quran 2:124-133, 3:33-34, 6:74-83, 9:114, 11:69-76, 14:35-41, 15:51-60, 19:41-49, 21:51-71, 22:26, 26:69-82, 29:16-27, 37:83-113, 43:26-28, 51:24-30, 53:37, 57:26, 60:4; Sahih al-Bukhari 3350, 3357, 3364, 3365, 3366; Sahih Muslim 2370

---

## 7. Lut (عليه السلام) — Lot

**Prophet to the People of Sodom**

Lut was Ibrahim's nephew and was sent to the people of Sodom and Gomorrah, who practiced homosexuality — the first nation to do so openly.

- **The People's Sin:** The people of Lut engaged in immoral acts never before committed by any people — approaching men with desire instead of women. *Quran 7:80-81, 27:54-55, 29:28-29*
- **The Message:** Lut warned his people and called them to righteousness. *Quran 26:160-166, 27:54*
- **The Angels' Visit:** Angels came to Lut in the form of handsome young men. The people rushed to Lut's house demanding the guests. *Quran 11:77-79, 15:61-70, 54:37*
- **The Punishment:** Allah rained down stones of baked clay upon them, overturning their cities. *Quran 7:84, 11:82-83, 15:73-74, 26:172-173, 27:58, 29:34, 54:33-36, 66:10*
- **Lut's Wife:** Lut's wife was among the destroyed — she did not believe. *Quran 66:10, 15:60*

**Sources:** Quran 7:80-84, 11:70-83, 15:57-77, 21:74-75, 26:160-175, 27:54-58, 29:26-35, 37:133-138, 51:31-37, 54:33-40, 66:10; Sahih al-Bukhari 4245, 6992; Sahih Muslim 2889

---

## 8. Ismail (عليه السلام) — Ishmael

**The First-Born Son of Ibrahim and Builder of the Ka'bah**

Ismail was the first son of Ibrahim, born to Hajar (Hagar). He is the forefather of the Arabs and the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).

- **The Desert:** Ibrahim left Hajar and the infant Ismail in the barren valley of Makkah at Allah's command. *Quran 14:37; Sahih al-Bukhari 3364*
- **Zamzam:** Hajar ran between Safa and Marwah searching for water. Allah caused the spring of Zamzam to gush forth beneath Ismail's feet. — **Sahih al-Bukhari 3364, 3365**
- **The Sacrifice:** Ismail is the son whom Ibrahim was commanded to sacrifice in a dream. Both father and son submitted. Allah ransomed him with a great sacrifice. *Quran 37:102-107*
- **Building the Ka'bah:** Ibrahim and Ismail together raised the foundations of the Ka'bah. *Quran 2:125-127*
- **Character:** Ismail is described as *sadiq al-wa'd* (true to his promise) and was a messenger and prophet. *Quran 19:54-55*

**Sources:** Quran 2:125-129, 2:133, 2:136, 3:84, 4:163, 6:86, 14:39, 19:54-55, 21:85, 38:48, 37:101-107; Sahih al-Bukhari 3364-3366

---

## 9. Ishaq (عليه السلام) — Isaac

**The Second Son of Ibrahim and Father of Yaqub**

Ishaq was born to Ibrahim and Sarah through a miraculous birth — angels gave them the glad tidings.

- **Glad Tidings:** The angels gave Ibrahim and Sarah the good news of a wise son, Ishaq. Sarah was astonished given their old age. *Quran 11:69-71, 15:51-55, 37:112, 51:28-30*
- **Miraculous Birth:** Allah describes Ishaq as a gift to Ibrahim in old age. *Quran 14:39, 37:112-113*
- **Blessing:** Allah bestowed blessings upon Ishaq and his progeny. *Quran 37:113, 19:49*

**Sources:** Quran 2:133, 2:136, 3:84, 4:163, 6:84, 11:71, 12:6, 14:39, 19:49, 21:72, 29:27, 37:112-113, 38:45; Sahih al-Bukhari 3344

---

## 10. Yaqub (عليه السلام) — Jacob

**Father of the Tribes (Bani Isra'il)**

Yaqub, also known as Isra'il, was the son of Ishaq and the father of twelve sons who became the twelve tribes of Bani Isra'il.

- **The Loss of Yusuf:** Yaqub suffered deeply when Yusuf was taken from him, and his eyes turned white with grief. *Quran 12:84*
- **Patience:** Yaqub is the exemplar of *sabr jamil* (beautiful patience). He said *"So patience is most fitting. And Allah is the one sought for help against that which you describe."* *Quran 12:18, 12:83*
- **Reunion:** Yaqub was reunited with Yusuf in Egypt after many years. *Quran 12:96-99, 12:100*
- **Legacy:** He instructed his sons on his deathbed to worship Allah alone. *Quran 2:133*

**Sources:** Quran 2:133, 2:136, 3:84, 4:163, 6:84, 12:4-18, 12:68-99, 19:49, 21:72, 29:27, 38:45; Sahih al-Bukhari 3380, 3389, 3430

---

## 11. Yusuf (عليه السلام) — Joseph

**The Exemplar of Beauty and Patience**

Yusuf's story is told in its entirety in Surah Yusuf (Chapter 12), the most detailed single prophet narrative in the Quran.

- **The Dream:** Yusuf saw eleven stars, the sun, and the moon prostrating to him. *Quran 12:4*
- **The Brothers' Jealousy:** His brothers plotted against him and threw him into a well. *Quran 12:7-10*
- **Sold into Slavery:** A caravan found him and sold him in Egypt for a cheap price. *Quran 12:19-20*
- **The Trial of Seduction:** The wife of the Aziz (minister) attempted to seduce him. Yusuf sought refuge with Allah and chose prison over sin. *Quran 12:23-27, 12:33*
- **Interpreting Dreams:** In prison, Yusuf interpreted the dreams of two companions. *Quran 12:36-41*
- **Rise to Power:** The king of Egypt had a dream that only Yusuf could interpret. Yusuf was elevated to the position of minister of finance/agriculture. *Quran 12:43-55*
- **The Reunion:** Through a series of events, Yusuf's brothers came to Egypt for food. Eventually, Yusuf revealed himself to them and brought his family to Egypt. *Quran 12:58-99*
- **The Prostration:** Yusuf's parents and brothers prostrated to him — the fulfillment of his childhood dream. *Quran 12:100*

**Sources:** Quran 12:1-111, 6:84, 40:34; Sahih al-Bukhari 3377, 3380, 3389, 3430

---

## 12. Shu'ayb (عليه السلام) — Jethro

**Prophet to the People of Madyan**

Shu'ayb was sent to the people of Madyan, who were corrupt merchants — cheating in trade and committing fraud.

- **The Message:** Shu'ayb called his people to worship Allah, give full measure and weight, and not defraud people. *Quran 7:85, 11:84-85, 26:181-183*
- **Rejection:** They mocked him and threatened to expel him. *Quran 7:88, 11:91, 26:185-187, 29:18*
- **The Punishment:** A terrible cry (sayhah) seized them, and they lay dead in their homes. *Quran 7:91-92, 11:94-95, 26:189, 29:36-37*
- **Also Sent to Ashab al-Aykah:** Some scholars consider Shu'ayb was also sent to the People of the Thicket (Aykah), who were punished by the shade of a day of painful torment. *Quran 26:176, 38:13, 50:14*

**Sources:** Quran 7:85-93, 9:70, 11:84-95, 22:44, 26:176-191, 29:36-37, 38:13, 50:14

---

## 13. Ayyub (عليه السلام) — Job

**The Exemplar of Patience in Suffering**

Ayyub was a wealthy and righteous man who was tested with the loss of his wealth, children, and health, yet remained patient.

- **The Trial:** Ayyub lost his wealth, his children, and was afflicted with a painful disease for many years. *Quran 38:41-44*
- **Patience:** Despite his severe suffering, Ayyub remained steadfast and never despaired of Allah's mercy. He said: *"Indeed, adversity has touched me, and You are the Most Merciful of the merciful."* *Quran 21:83*
- **The Recovery:** Allah restored Ayyub's health, gave him double his former wealth, and reunited him with his family. *Quran 38:42-44, 21:84*
- **The Wife's Devotion:** In a hadith, the Prophet (SAW) said Ayyub's wife remained with him and cared for him through his illness. — **Sahih al-Bukhari 3391**
- **The Spring:** Allah told Ayyub to strike the ground with his foot, and a spring of cool water emerged for him to drink and wash. *Quran 38:42*

**Sources:** Quran 4:163, 6:84, 21:83-84, 38:41-44; Sahih al-Bukhari 3391

---

## 14. Dhul-Kifl (عليه السلام)

**A Prophet of Patience**

Dhul-Kifl is mentioned twice in the Quran alongside other prophets, but his story is brief.

- **Mention:** Allah mentions him among the patient and righteous. *Quran 21:85, 38:48*
- **Scholarly Debate:** Some scholars identify Dhul-Kifl with various biblical figures (Ezekiel, Obadiah, etc.), while others say he was a prophet from Bani Isra'il known for his patience in fulfilling a pledge.
- **The Pledge:** A narration mentions that a prophet offered to take on the responsibility of judging and caring for his people, and Dhul-Kifl volunteered and fulfilled it with patience. — **Mentioned in Tafsir with varying chains**

**Sources:** Quran 21:85, 38:48

---

## 15. Musa (عليه السلام) — Moses

**Kaleemullah — The One Who Spoke to Allah**

Musa is the most frequently mentioned prophet in the Quran, appearing in over 130 verses across numerous surahs. He is among the Ulul 'Azm.

- **Birth and Rescue:** Fir'awn (Pharaoh) ordered the killing of all male Israelite children. Musa's mother was inspired to place him in a basket in the river. He was found by Fir'awn's wife, Asiyah, who adopted him. *Quran 20:37-40, 28:7-13*
- **The Killing:** As a young man, Musa unintentionally killed an Egyptian who was oppressing an Israelite. He fled to Madyan in fear. *Quran 28:14-21*
- **In Madyan:** Musa helped two women water their flock. Their father (Shu'ayb, according to many scholars) offered Musa employment and one of his daughters in marriage. *Quran 28:22-28*
- **The Burning Bush:** On his return to Egypt, Musa saw a fire on Mount Tur. Allah spoke to him from the fire, commissioning him as a prophet and messenger. *Quran 20:9-16, 27:7-14, 28:29-31*
- **The Miracles:** Allah gave Musa two signs — his staff that turned into a serpent, and his hand that turned white without disease. *Quran 7:107-108, 20:17-23, 26:32-33, 28:31-32*
- **Confrontation with Fir'awn:** Musa and Harun went to Fir'awn and called him to worship Allah. *Quran 7:103-108, 20:42-48, 26:10-17*
- **The Magicians:** Fir'awn's magicians competed with Musa. When Musa's staff devoured their illusions, the magicians believed and proclaimed faith in the Lord of Musa and Harun. *Quran 7:113-122, 20:66-73, 26:38-51*
- **The Plagues:** Allah sent signs and plagues upon Fir'awn and his people. *Quran 7:130-133*
- **The Exodus:** Musa led Bani Isra'il out of Egypt. *Quran 26:52-66*
- **The Parting of the Sea:** Fir'awn pursued them. Allah commanded Musa to strike the sea with his staff, and it split into twelve paths. Fir'awn and his army were drowned. *Quran 2:50, 7:136, 10:90-92, 20:77-78, 26:52-68, 28:37-40, 44:24-29*
- **The Sinai Covenant:** Musa went to Mount Sinai to receive the Tawrat (Torah). *Quran 2:63-66, 7:143, 7:154*
- **The Golden Calf:** In Musa's absence, the Samiri led Bani Isra'il to worship a golden calf. Musa was furious and destroyed the calf. *Quran 2:51, 7:148-154, 20:83-98*
- **Speaking to Allah:** Musa asked to see Allah. Allah showed him a glimpse of His glory, and the mountain crumbled. Musa fell unconscious. *Quran 7:143*
- **The Story of Al-Khidr:** Musa traveled with a mysterious servant of Allah (Al-Khidr) to learn deeper wisdom. *Quran 18:60-82; Sahih al-Bukhari 122, 2728, 3278*
- **Qarun (Korah):** A wealthy but arrogant man from Bani Isra'il who was swallowed by the earth. *Quran 28:76-82*

**Sources:** Quran 2:49-61, 2:67-71, 4:153-154, 5:20-26, 6:84, 7:103-171, 10:75-93, 11:96-99, 14:5-8, 17:101-104, 18:60-82, 20:9-97, 23:45-49, 25:35, 26:10-68, 27:7-14, 28:2-44, 28:76-82, 29:39, 32:23, 37:114-122, 40:23-46, 43:46-56, 44:30-33, 46:30, 51:38-40, 53:36, 61:5; Sahih al-Bukhari 122, 2728, 3278, 3213, 3217, 3227, 3329, 3405, 4400, 4510, 4680, 4682, 4683, 4685, 4686, 4687, 4693, 4694, 4695, 4696, 4697, 4699, 4718, 4719, 4720; Sahih Muslim 2372, 2736

## 16. Harun (عليه السلام) — Aaron

**The Brother and Spokesman of Musa**

Harun was Musa's elder brother, his partner in prophethood, and his spokesman to Fir'awn.

- **The Appointment:** Allah appointed Harun as a prophet and as Musa's helper and spokesman. *Quran 19:53, 20:29-36, 25:35, 28:34-35*
- **Before Fir'awn:** Harun stood with Musa before Fir'awn, delivering the message. *Quran 7:103-104, 10:75-76*
- **Guardian During Absence:** When Musa went to Mount Sinai, he left Harun in charge of Bani Isra'il. *Quran 7:142*
- **The Golden Calf:** Harun tried to stop the people from worshipping the calf but was overpowered and nearly killed. *Quran 7:150, 20:90-94*

**Sources:** Quran 2:248, 4:163, 6:84, 7:122, 7:142, 10:75, 19:28, 19:53, 20:30, 20:42, 21:48, 23:45, 25:35, 26:48, 28:34-35, 37:114-120, 38:26; Sahih al-Bukhari 4482

---

## 17. Dawud (عليه السلام) — David

**Prophet, King, and Recipient of the Zabur**

Dawud was both a prophet and a king — the first in Bani Isra'il to combine both roles. He was given the Zabur (Psalms).

- **The Duel with Jalut (Goliath):** Dawud, a young boy, defeated the giant warrior Jalut in combat. *Quran 2:249-251*
- **The Zabur:** Allah gave Dawud the Zabur (Psalms). *Quran 4:163, 17:55*
- **The Mountains and Birds:** Allah subjected the mountains and birds to glorify Allah with Dawud. *Quran 21:79, 34:10, 38:18-19*
- **Iron Softened:** Allah made iron soft for Dawud, enabling him to make coats of armor. *Quran 21:80, 34:10-11*
- **Justice:** Dawud was given wisdom and sound judgment. *Quran 38:20, 21:78-79*
- **The Two Litigants:** Two men came to Dawud seeking judgment (one with 99 ewes, the other with one). *Quran 38:21-26*
- **Repentance:** Dawud sought Allah's forgiveness and fell down in prostration. *Quran 38:24-25*
- **The Prophet (SAW) on Dawud's Fasting:** The Prophet (SAW) said Dawud used to fast alternate days, which is the most beloved fasting to Allah. — **Sahih al-Bukhari 1131, Sahih Muslim 1159**

**Sources:** Quran 2:251, 4:163, 5:78, 6:84, 17:55, 21:78-80, 27:15-16, 34:10-11, 38:17-26; Sahih al-Bukhari 1131, 3213; Sahih Muslim 1159

---

## 18. Sulayman (عليه السلام) — Solomon

**The King Who Commanded Wind, Jinn, and Animals**

Sulayman inherited Dawud's kingdom and was given even greater power and wisdom.

- **Inheritance and Wisdom:** Sulayman inherited from Dawud and was given immense wisdom. *Quran 27:15-16, 38:30*
- **The Wind:** The wind was subjected to Sulayman, carrying him wherever he wished. *Quran 21:81, 34:12, 38:36*
- **The Jinn:** Jinn worked for Sulayman, building palaces, statues, and large basins. *Quran 21:82, 34:12-13, 38:37*
- **The Language of Birds and Ants:** Sulayman understood the speech of birds and animals. The famous incident of the ant who warned her colony. *Quran 27:16-19*
- **The Hoopoe and Bilqis:** The hoopoe bird brought news of the Queen of Saba (Bilqis) who worshipped the sun. Sulayman invited her to Islam. *Quran 27:20-44*
- **The Throne:** One who had knowledge of the Book brought Bilqis's throne to Sulayman in the blink of an eye. *Quran 27:38-40*
- **Bilqis's Submission:** The Queen submitted to Allah, the Lord of the worlds. *Quran 27:44*
- **The Horse Parade:** Sulayman's love for his fine horses until the sun set. *Quran 38:31-33*
- **The Body on the Throne:** When Sulayman died, he remained leaning on his staff for a long time, and the jinn continued working, not knowing he had died, until a termite ate through his staff and he fell. *Quran 34:14*

**Sources:** Quran 2:102, 4:163, 6:84, 21:78-82, 27:15-44, 34:12-14, 38:30-40; Sahih al-Bukhari 3213, 3424, 4682; Sahih Muslim 1774

---

## 19. Ilyas (عليه السلام) — Elijah

**Prophet to the People of Baal**

Ilyas was sent to the people of Isra'il who had turned to idol worship, specifically the worship of Baal.

- **The Message:** Ilyas called his people to worship Allah alone and abandon the idol Baal. *Quran 37:123-126*
- **Rejection:** His people denied him. *Quran 37:127*
- **Punishment:** They were destined for punishment except the sincere servants of Allah. *Quran 37:128*
- **Among the Righteous:** Allah mentions Ilyas among the righteous. *Quran 6:85*

**Sources:** Quran 6:85, 37:123-132

---

## 20. Alyasa' (عليه السلام) — Elisha

**A Prophet of Great Blessing**

Alyasa' is mentioned briefly but alongside the greatest prophets.

- **Mention:** Allah includes Alyasa' among the chosen, the righteous. *Quran 6:86, 38:48*
- **Relationship to Ilyas:** Most scholars identify Alyasa' as a disciple and successor of Ilyas, though the Quran does not detail his story.
- **Among the Best:** He is mentioned in the company of Ibrahim, Musa, Ismail, and Ilyas — an honorable rank. *Quran 38:48-49*

**Sources:** Quran 6:86, 38:48

---

## 21. Yunus (عليه السلام) — Jonah

**Dhun-Nun — The Man of the Whale**

Yunus is the exemplar of calling upon Allah in the depths of despair.

- **The Departure:** Yunus left his people in anger before Allah gave him permission. *Quran 21:87, 37:139-140, 68:48*
- **The Whale:** He boarded a ship, lots were drawn, and he was cast into the sea. A whale swallowed him. *Quran 37:139-142, 68:48-49*
- **The Supplication:** In the belly of the whale, Yunus made his famous dua: *"There is no deity except You; exalted are You. Indeed, I have been of the wrongdoers." (La ilaha illa Anta, Subhanaka inni kuntu minaz-zalimin)* *Quran 21:87*
- **The Rescue:** Allah answered him and saved him from distress. *Quran 21:88, 37:143-144*
- **The Gourd Plant:** Allah caused a gourd plant to grow over him for shade while he recovered. *Quran 37:146*
- **His People Believed:** Unlike other nations, Yunus's people believed after seeing the signs, and they were spared. *Quran 10:98, 37:148*
- **The Prophet's (SAW) Advice:** The Prophet (SAW) said: *"Do not prefer me over Yunus ibn Matta."* — **Sahih al-Bukhari 3412, Sahih Muslim 4375**

**Sources:** Quran 4:163, 6:86, 10:98, 21:87-88, 37:139-148, 68:48-50, 37:139; Sahih al-Bukhari 3412, 3413; Sahih Muslim 4375

---

## 22. Zakariyya (عليه السلام) — Zechariah

**The Father of Yahya in Old Age**

Zakariyya was a prophet from Bani Isra'il and the guardian of Maryam (Mary).

- **The Prayer:** Zakariyya prayed to Allah for a righteous child despite his old age and his wife's barrenness. *Quran 3:38, 19:2-6, 21:89*
- **The Glad Tidings:** While standing in prayer in the mihrab, angels gave him news of a son named Yahya. *Quran 3:39, 19:7, 21:90*
- **The Sign:** Zakariyya asked for a sign. Allah told him he would not speak to people for three nights despite being healthy. *Quran 3:41, 19:10*
- **Guardian of Maryam:** Zakariyya was chosen to care for Maryam. *Quran 3:37, 3:44*

**Sources:** Quran 3:37-41, 6:85, 19:2-15, 21:89-90

---

## 23. Yahya (عليه السلام) — John the Baptist

**The Prophet Born to Elderly Parents**

Yahya was the son of Zakariyya, given wisdom as a child.

- **The Name:** Allah gave him the name Yahya, a name not previously given to anyone. *Quran 19:7*
- **Wisdom in Youth:** Yahya was given wisdom, judgment, and compassion from his parents while still a child. *Quran 19:12-14*
- **Purity:** He is described as *hananan* (tenderness/purity) from Us, and *zakiyyan* (pure). *Quran 19:13*
- **Righteousness:** He was righteous, dutiful to his parents, and not arrogant or disobedient. *Quran 19:14*
- **Peace Upon Him:** *"And peace be upon him the day he was born and the day he dies and the day he is raised alive."* *Quran 19:15*

**Sources:** Quran 3:39, 6:85, 19:7-15, 21:90

---

## 24. Isa (عليه السلام) — Jesus

**Ruhullah and Kalimatullah — The Spirit and Word of Allah**

Isa ibn Maryam is one of the Ulul 'Azm. He was born miraculously without a father and was given remarkable miracles by Allah.

- **The Miraculous Birth:** The angel Jibril came to Maryam with the news of a pure son. She was astonished: *"How can I have a son when no man has touched me?"* The reply: *"Thus Allah creates what He wills."* *Quran 3:45-47, 19:16-21, 21:91, 66:12*
- **The Cradle Miracle:** Isa spoke from the cradle as an infant, declaring his prophethood. *Quran 19:29-33*
- **The Miracles:** By Allah's permission, Isa:
  - Fashioned a bird from clay and breathed life into it *Quran 3:49, 5:110*
  - Healed the blind and the leper *Quran 3:49, 5:110*
  - Raised the dead *Quran 3:49, 5:110*
  - Informed people of what they ate and stored in their homes *Quran 3:49*
  - A table of food sent from heaven for the disciples *Quran 5:112-115*
- **The Injil (Gospel):** Allah gave Isa the Injil as guidance and light. *Quran 5:46, 57:27*
- **The Disciples (Hawariyyun):** Isa had devoted companions who believed in him. *Quran 3:52-53, 5:111, 61:14*
- **NOT Crucified:** The Quran explicitly states that Isa was neither killed nor crucified — it was made to appear so. *Quran 4:157-158*
- **Raised to Allah:** Allah raised Isa to Himself. *Quran 3:55, 4:158*
- **The Second Coming:** According to authentic hadith, Isa will return to Earth before the Day of Judgment, fight the Dajjal (Antichrist), and rule with justice. — **Sahih al-Bukhari 3448, 2222; Sahih Muslim 155, 2937**
- **Status:** He is called *Kalimatullah* (Word of Allah) and *Ruhullah* (Spirit from Allah) — *Quran 3:45, 4:171*. He is among the Ulul 'Azm.

**Sources:** Quran 2:87, 2:136, 2:253, 3:45-59, 3:84, 4:157-159, 4:171-172, 5:46-47, 5:72-78, 5:110-115, 5:116-118, 6:85, 19:16-40, 21:91, 23:50, 33:7, 42:13, 43:57-65, 57:27, 61:6, 61:14; Sahih al-Bukhari 2222, 3448, 4496; Sahih Muslim 155, 243, 2937

## 25. Muhammad (صلى الله عليه وسلم) — Muhammad

**Khatam an-Nabiyyin — The Seal of the Prophets**

Muhammad (SAW) is the final prophet and messenger, sent as a mercy to all the worlds.

- **Lineage:** From the tribe of Quraysh, the family of Hashim, descended from Ibrahim through Ismail. *Quran 3:68, 4:54*
- **Birth:** Born in Makkah in the Year of the Elephant (~570 CE). Orphaned at a young age — his father Abdullah died before his birth, and his mother Aminah died when he was six. — **Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim biographical narrations**
- **Upbringing:** Raised by his grandfather Abdul Muttalib, then his uncle Abu Talib. — **Seerah narrations**
- **The Trustworthy:** Known as *Al-Amin* (the trustworthy) even before prophethood. He resolved the dispute of placing the Black Stone (Hajr al-Aswad) during the Ka'bah's reconstruction. — **Recorded in authentic Seerah**
- **Marriage to Khadijah:** Married Khadijah bint Khuwaylid, a wealthy merchant, who was the first to believe in him. — **Sahih al-Bukhari 3878**
- **The First Revelation (610 CE):** At age 40, while meditating in the Cave of Hira, the angel Jibril came to him with the first verses: *"Read in the name of your Lord who created..."* (Surah Al-'Alaq, 96:1-5). — **Sahih al-Bukhari 3, 4953; Sahih Muslim 160**
- **The Message:** He called the people of Makkah to worship Allah alone, abandon idolatry, and establish justice and morality. *Quran 7:158, 21:107, 34:28*
- **Persecution:** The early Muslims faced severe persecution in Makkah. Some were tortured, and many were boycotted. — **Sahih al-Bukhari 3853, 3904**
- **The Night Journey and Ascension (Isra and Mi'raj):** The Prophet (SAW) was taken from Makkah to Jerusalem (Al-Aqsa) and then ascended through the heavens, meeting previous prophets and receiving the five daily prayers. *Quran 17:1, 53:1-18; Sahih al-Bukhari 3207, 3430, 3887; Sahih Muslim 162, 164, 2380, 4374*
- **The Hijrah (622 CE):** The Prophet (SAW) migrated from Makkah to Madinah, marking the start of the Islamic calendar. *Quran 9:40; Sahih al-Bukhari 3900-3906*
- **The Battles:** Badr (624 CE) — *Quran 3:123; 8:1-19; 8:41-45*; Uhud (625 CE) — *Quran 3:121-175*; Ahzab/Trench (627 CE) — *Quran 33:9-27*
- **Treaty of Hudaybiyyah (628 CE):** A peace treaty that the Quran called a "clear victory." *Quran 48:1-27; Sahih al-Bukhari 2731-2733*
- **The Conquest of Makkah (630 CE):** The Prophet (SAW) returned to Makkah and conquered it with minimal bloodshed, forgiving his former enemies. — **Sahih al-Bukhari 4280-4297**
- **The Farewell Pilgrimage (632 CE):** The Prophet (SAW) delivered his Farewell Sermon at Arafat, establishing the rights and duties of Muslims. *Quran 5:3 ("This day I have perfected for you your religion..."); Sahih Muslim 1218*
- **Death (632 CE):** The Prophet (SAW) passed away in Madinah on the 12th of Rabi' al-Awwal, 11 AH. — **Sahih al-Bukhari 4444, 4449; Sahih Muslim 438**
- **The Final Prophet:** *"Muhammad is not the father of any of your men, but he is the Messenger of Allah and the seal of the prophets."* *Quran 33:40*
- **A Mercy to the Worlds:** *"And We have not sent you, [O Muhammad], except as a mercy to the worlds."* *Quran 21:107*
- **Universal Message:** *"And We have not sent you except comprehensively to mankind as a bringer of good tidings and a warner."* *Quran 34:28*

**Sources:** Quran 2:119, 2:151, 3:31, 3:144, 3:164, 4:79-80, 5:15-16, 5:19, 6:50, 7:157-158, 7:188, 9:40, 9:117, 10:2, 14:1, 17:1, 17:105, 18:110, 21:107, 25:1, 25:56, 26:194, 28:46, 33:21, 33:40, 33:45-46, 34:28, 36:2-4, 41:2-6, 42:7, 48:1-5, 48:8-10, 48:29, 49:2-3, 53:1-18, 62:2, 65:10-11, 68:4, 73:1-5, 80:1-10, 93:1-11, 94:1-8, 96:1-5, 108:1-3, 110:1-3, 111:1-5; Sahih al-Bukhari (numerous); Sahih Muslim (numerous)

## The Ulul 'Azm — The Five Prophets of Determination

These five prophets are distinguished by their extraordinary perseverance and are mentioned together in the Quran:

> *"He has ordained for you of religion what He enjoined upon Nuh and that which We have revealed to you, [O Muhammad], and what We enjoined upon Ibrahim and Musa and Isa — to establish the religion and not be divided therein."*  
> — **Quran 42:13**

1. **Nuh (AS)** — 950 years of preaching with few followers
2. **Ibrahim (AS)** — Thrown into fire, tested with the sacrifice of his son
3. **Musa (AS)** — Confronted the most powerful tyrant, led a nation through the sea
4. **Isa (AS)** — Faced plots against his life, given miraculous birth and powers
5. **Muhammad (SAW)** — Endured persecution, warfare, and the burden of the final message
